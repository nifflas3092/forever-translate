# Forever Translate

**Read foreign chat in WoW: Forever, and answer back in their language.**

### Works on its own: slang hints
Common WoW words in Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, German and French get a
short grey English hint right in your chat window:

> [Trade] [Pedro]: alguien para dg? falta tanque *(EN: anyone, dungeon, need, tank)*

No setup, no internet, nothing to install. Type `/ftr gloss` to turn it off.

### Full translation (optional free companion app)
For whole sentences, add the free **Forever Translate** desktop app for
Windows. It's open source and available from the project's GitHub page
(link in the Source section of this page). The app:

- translates every foreign message into your language (taken from your WoW
  client automatically)
- shows it in a window styled like the in-game chat frame, with All / Party /
  Guild / Whispers / World tabs
- translates your replies into the other player's language and copies them,
  so you just press Enter, Ctrl+V, Enter in game

Addons can't use the internet, so the addon shows each message as a small
strip of coloured squares in a corner of the screen and the app reads it.
The app never touches the game's files or memory and never types for you.
Run WoW in Windowed or Windowed (Fullscreen) mode.

### Privacy and safety
- No accounts, ads or tracking. Nothing is ever sent to the author.
- Chat is sent only to the free translation service you choose (MyMemory
  by default; DeepL, Claude and Google are optional).
- Text from other players is only displayed, never run. Copied replies can
  never start with a slash command.
- **Streamers:** the strip shows the chat it sends, including whispers.
  Use `/ftr off` while live, or `/ftr channel whisper` to leave whispers out.

### Commands
`/ftr` shows them all: `on`, `off`, `gloss`, `move`, `reset`, `test`, and
`channel <name>`.
